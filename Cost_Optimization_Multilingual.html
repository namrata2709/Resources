AWS Pricing Key Principles

aws© 2025 Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Pricing Key Principles
Understand the fundamentals of pricing
Three fundamental drivers: compute, storage, and outbound data transfer. These are billed independently and transparently.
Start early with cost optimization
Trade fixed expenses (data centers, physical servers) for variable expenses — pay only for what you consume.
Maximize the power of flexibility
AWS services are priced independently, available on-demand — choose and pay for exactly what you need, when you need it.
Use the right pricing model for the job
On-Demand, Savings Plans, Spot Instances, Reserved Instances — match your workload pattern to the right model.

AWS Cost Optimization

aws© 2025 Amazon Web Services, Inc.
What is AWS Cost Optimization?
Choose the Right Pricing Models
  • Reserved Instances for RDS, Redshift, ElastiCache & OpenSearch
  • Spot Instances to reduce EC2 costs
  • Compute Savings Plans for EC2, Fargate & Lambda
  • Savings Plans require 1 or 3-year commitment
Match Capacity with Demand
  • Right-size or stop low-utilisation EC2 instances
  • Stop low-utilisation RDS; pause Redshift
  • DynamoDB: Auto Scaling or On-Demand mode
  • Use AWS Compute Optimizer for recommendations
Implement Processes to Identify Waste
  • Delete low-utilisation EBS volumes (snapshot first)
  • Move S3 objects to cheaper tiers (Lifecycle policies)
  • Remove idle load balancers & unused IPs
  • Use AWS Cost Explorer & Budgets for visibility
aws© 2025 Amazon Web Services, Inc. or its affiliates. All rights reserved.
AWS Cost Optimization Tools & Services

💰 AWS Cost Explorer

Visualise, understand and manage your AWS costs and usage over time. Filter by service, region, tag. Identify top cost drivers instantly.

📊 AWS Budgets

Set custom cost and usage budgets. Receive alerts via SNS or email when thresholds are exceeded — daily, monthly or annual periods.

🔍 AWS Compute Optimizer

ML-based recommendations for EC2, Lambda, EBS and ECS on Fargate. Identifies over- and under-provisioned resources automatically.

🛡️ AWS Trusted Advisor

Real-time guidance across cost optimisation, security, fault tolerance and performance. Highlights idle and underutilised resources.

📋 AWS Cost & Usage Report (CUR)

Most granular billing data available. Integrates with Athena, Redshift or QuickSight for custom analytics and showback/chargeback.

🏷️ AWS Cost Allocation Tags

Tag resources by project, team or environment. Use tags in Cost Explorer and CUR to attribute spend to the right business unit.
