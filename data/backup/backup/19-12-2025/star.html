⭐ STAR method — very simple

STAR is a simple way to answer “experience” or “behavioural” interview questions.

It helps you answer clearly, in order, without confusion.

STAR = 4 steps

S – Situation

What was the situation?
(Where? When? What problem?)

👉 1–2 lines only

T – Task

What was your responsibility?
(What were you supposed to do?)

👉 1 line

A – Action

What did YOU do?
(Steps you took, tools you used)

👉 Most important part

R – Result

What was the outcome?
(What improved? What did you learn?)

👉 End positive

Very small example (easy)

Question: “Tell me about a problem you solved.”

S:
“In one sprint, deployment was failing often.”

T:
“I had to fix the deployment issues.”

A:
“I automated the deployment using a CI/CD pipeline and added basic tests.”

R:
“Deployments became stable and releases were on time.”

One line to remember

STAR = What happened → What I had to do → What I did → What happened finally
