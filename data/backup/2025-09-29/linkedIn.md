**When to post:**
- Tuesday-Thursday: 8-10 AM (morning commute)
- Tuesday-Thursday: 12-2 PM (lunch break)
- Avoid Mondays (inbox catch-up) and Fridays (weekend mode)

**Content strategy:**
- Lead with a surprising statistic or question
- Use personal story (the import-export business)
- Break down complex topics simply
- Include specific, actionable value
- End with clear call-to-action
- Provide free resources (no paywall)

- Consider creating a custom LinkedIn image:
