What is Multi-Factor Authentication?

MFA: Security method requiring TWO forms of verification to log in

🏠 Think of it like: Password = House key | MFA Code = Alarm system

Even if someone steals your key, they can't get past the alarm!

Result: Hackers would need your password AND your phone = Nearly impossible to breach

How MFA Works

Login Flow WITH MFA:

Step 1: Enter Email → ✓
Step 2: Enter Password → ✓
Step 3: AWS asks: "What's your 6-digit code?"
Step 4: Check phone app → Code: 582 937
Step 5: Enter code → ✓
Step 6: Access Granted! 🎉

Without BOTH password AND phone = ❌ Access Denied

Protection Level: 99.9% of account breaches are prevented by MFA

Let's Set Up MFA! 🚀

Switching to AWS Console...

Follow along step-by-step!

MFA Setup Steps

  1. AWS Console → Account Name → Security Credentials
  2. Scroll to "Multi-factor authentication"
  3. Click "Assign MFA device"
  4. Name: "MyPhone-MFA" | Type: Authenticator app
  5. Download Google/Microsoft Authenticator app
  6. Scan QR code with phone
  7. Enter TWO consecutive codes → Done!

Budget Alerts = Your Safety Net

What Budget Alerts Do:

✅ Monitor your spending 24/7 automatically

✅ Email you BEFORE charges get out of control

✅ Give you time to investigate and stop resources

✅ Protect you from $1,000+ surprise bills

Setup Time: 5 minutes | Protection: Priceless

Let's Set Up Budget Alerts! 💰

Back to AWS Console...

Billing Dashboard → Budgets

Budget Setup Steps

  1. Search "Billing" → Billing and Cost Management
  2. Left sidebar → Budgets → Create budget
  3. Choose: Cost budget → Next
  4. Name: "Monthly-Free-Tier-Budget"
  5. Amount: $10 | Period: Monthly
  6. Alert 1: 80% ($8) → Email you
  7. Alert 2: 100% ($10) → Email you
  8. Review → Create budget → Done!

Why TWO Alerts?

🟡 Alert 1 at 80% ($8):

"Warning! Check what's running. You still have $2 buffer."

Action: Review your resources, identify unexpected charges

🔴 Alert 2 at 100% ($10):

"EMERGENCY! You've hit your budget limit!"

Action: Stop everything, investigate immediately

💡 Pro Tip: First alert = Investigation | Second alert = Emergency stop

🎓 Quick Knowledge Check

Q: What are the TWO factors in MFA?

A: (1) Something you KNOW (password) + (2) Something you HAVE (phone with code)

Q: When should you enable MFA?

A: Immediately after creating AWS account - before doing anything else

Q: What happens at 100% budget threshold?

A: You receive alert email, but AWS does NOT automatically stop services
