## Question Format Version

❓ **Quick AWS quiz: If your EC2 instance gets hacked because you used password "12345", who is responsible - you or AWS?**

The answer reveals everything about the Shared Responsibility Model, one of the MOST tested concepts on AWS exams.

**The answer:** YOU are responsible. Here's why:

AWS secures the infrastructure (data centers, hardware, hypervisor), but YOU must secure:
→ Your passwords and access control
→ OS patches (in IaaS like EC2)
→ Security group configurations
→ Your data and encryption
